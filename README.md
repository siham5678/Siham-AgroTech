# Siham-AgroTech# Siham Agrotech

A pilot-interest website for **Siham Agrotech**, a proposed Ethiopian agricultural technology company focused on machinery access, solar irrigation and operator training.

**Live site:** https://siham5678.github.io/Siham-AgroTech

> **Status: proposed start-up, not yet operational.** This website collects pilot interest only. It takes no payments, lists no prices and does not book machines.

---

## The problem

Most Ethiopian smallholder farmers cannot afford to buy a tractor or a solar irrigation pump, and farm work still relies mainly on oxen and hand tools. The business proposal behind this project also notes that only a minority of farmers irrigate, that many pump with costly diesel, and that most Ethiopians do not have a smartphone.

## The idea

Siham Agrotech is planned as a **service company**, not an equipment shop:

- **Machine plus trained operator:** farmers pay for the work done (plowing, planting, harvesting and similar jobs).
- **Equipment rental for certified farmers:** farmers who complete the training can rent equipment and operate it themselves.
- **Solar irrigation:** equipment sales and installation, lease-to-own, and irrigation-as-a-service.
- **Training and certification:** safe machine operation, basic repair and solar pump installation, for farmers and rural youth.
- **Many ways to book:** app, phone call, SMS, USSD and local agents, because app-only services would leave most farmers out.

## What this website does

- Explains the planned services and training tracks
- Lets a farmer prepare a **pilot-interest message** and send it by WhatsApp 
- Shows which contact channels are available now and which are only planned
- Links to **MELA**, the free farm decision helper

## Technologies used

- **HTML, CSS and JavaScript** in a single file (`index.html`)
- **GitHub Pages** for free hosting
- No server, database or paid service

The code is arranged in four small parts: configuration (contact number and links), text (ready for more languages), data (services, training, channels) and logic (builds the request message).

## How to run it

**Easiest:** open the live link above.

**On your own computer:** download `index.html` and double-click it, or open it with Chrome.

## Honest limits

- **Siham Agrotech does not operate yet.** The site describes a plan.
- **No prices are shown**, because no confirmed price list exists for these services.
- **Course lengths and partner institutions are not decided.**
- Figures in the business proposal come from the research cited there, or are marked as planning assumptions.
- Only the website and WhatsApp or copied messages are available today. Call center, SMS, USSD, agents and app are planned.
